Lyrics that bloom into constellations

VerseFlow follows your music through Linux MPRIS media players and lights up each lyric, one word at a time. Maintained by rajm012.

Linux portable build Source on GitHub
Follows Spotify, VLC and supported browser players through MPRIS.

Three ways to see your lyrics

Pick a style from the tray panel at any time.

☰

Stack

Three lines glide up as the song moves on, with a karaoke wipe across every word.

◎

Focus

One line at a time. Upcoming words float up into place just before they’re sung.

✦

Constellation

Fullscreen. Every word becomes a star, linked into a web, inside a soft ambient glow.

Run on your Linux desktop

DownloadExtract the Linux portable archive when available, or use the Linux source checkout.
Run itFrom source: run ./setup.sh, then ./run.sh. Portable builds include install.sh for your user account.
Play a songUse the control panel or tray icon to choose Stack, Focus, or Constellation.
Desktop support: Ubuntu/GNOME on X11 is the first target. Wayland overlay placement depends on the compositor. When no tray is available, the controls open in a regular window.
