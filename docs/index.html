GOOD TO KNOW

Before you press play.

Which players work with VerseFlow?+

Players that publish MPRIS metadata on your Linux session, including Spotify and VLC. Browser support depends on the browser and website. A generic media title may not match a song.

Where do the lyrics come from?+

Choose from lrc.red, BiniLyrics, LyricsPlus, KuGou, BetterLyrics, Musixmatch, and LRCLIB. Availability varies. Musixmatch needs your own API key and is disabled by default. Line-only lyrics use estimated word timing.

Does it work on Wayland?+

Fullscreen works as a regular application window. Desktop placement, click-through behaviour, and stacking depend on your compositor. The primary target is Ubuntu / GNOME on X11; full Wayland parity has not been verified.

What data leaves my desktop?+

Track titles, artists, and supported durations go to the enabled lyric providers. Artwork may be downloaded from the player's URL. Update checks contact GitHub. Lyrics are cached in memory, and settings stay in your XDG config directory.
